# Email Slicer Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026

Email Slicer does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information or Figma document content with the plugin developer or third parties.

The plugin processes the selected frames and slices inside Figma so it can create slice nodes and export images. Exported files are saved only to the location chosen by the user. Slicing and export settings are stored locally through Figma's client storage so they can be restored in later sessions.

Email Slicer has no external network access, analytics, advertising, cookies, tracking, third-party SDKs, external accounts, or payment processing.

Figma's own handling of account and document data is governed by Figma's terms and privacy policy.

For privacy or support questions, open an issue at
https://github.com/AadilShrestha/figmaSlicer/issues.
