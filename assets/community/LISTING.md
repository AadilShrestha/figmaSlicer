# Email Slicer — Community listing copy

## 1. Describe your resource

**Name**

Email Slicer

**Tagline**

Slice email frames into clean, export-ready images.

**Description**

Turn long email designs into production-ready image slices without leaving Figma.

Email Slicer creates real Figma Slice nodes across one or multiple selected frames. Cut by clicked layers, horizontal guides, equal parts, or maximum height. Preview every boundary, adjust slices directly on the canvas, then export exactly what you see as JPG or PNG—individually or in one ZIP.

• Live single- and multi-frame selection
• Remembers your export and slicing settings
• Supports top and bottom trim areas
• Preserves manual slice edits and hand-drawn slices
• Runs entirely offline with no account or network access

Select your email frame, choose a slicing method, click Slice, adjust if needed, then Export slices.

Design tools
